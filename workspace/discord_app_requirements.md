# Discord App Requirements (Verified 2026-08-05)

## 1. Discord Developer Terms of Service
**Source:** [Discord Developer Terms of Service](https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/8562894815383-Discord-Developer-Terms-of-Service)
**Effective Date:** July 8, 2024 (Last Updated: June 6, 2024)

### Key Requirements
- **Acceptance of Terms**: By using Discord’s APIs, you agree to comply with the Developer Terms, Developer Policy, Terms of Service, and Community Guidelines.
- **Registration & Application Information**: You must provide accurate and up-to-date information about yourself and your Application. Misrepresentation or masking of identity is prohibited.
- **License Grant**: Discord grants a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to use the APIs solely for integrating with and operating your Application.
- **Restrictions**: You may not:
  - Reverse engineer, copy, or redistribute the APIs.
  - Modify or create derivative works from the APIs.
  - Use the APIs for illegal, dangerous, or harmful activities.
  - Bypass Discord’s privacy, safety, or security features.
  - Request or collect user passwords or credentials.
  - Contact users without explicit permission.
  - Target users under the age of 13 or the minimum age required by law.

## 2. Discord Developer Policy
**Source:** [Discord Developer Policy](https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/8563934450327-Discord-Developer-Policy)
**Effective Date:** July 8, 2024 (Last Updated: June 6, 2024)

### Key Requirements
- **Monetization**: All Applications attempting to monetize must comply with Discord’s Monetization Terms and Policy. Paid features must be offered through Discord’s Premium Apps products at prices no higher than those offered elsewhere.
- **User Protection**: 
  - Do not modify a user’s account without explicit permission.
  - Do not initiate processes on a user or server’s behalf without permission.
  - Respect user privacy and opt-out decisions.
  - Do not collect, solicit, or deceive users into providing passwords or credentials.
  - Do not contact users without explicit permission (e.g., unsolicited DMs).
  - Do not target users with advertisements or marketing.
  - Do not contact users outside of Discord without permission.
- **Platform Rules**:
  - Do not use the APIs for illegal, dangerous, or harmful activities.
  - Do not direct your Application to users under the age of 13 or the minimum age required by law.

## 3. App Directory: App Content Requirements Policy
**Source:** [App Directory: App Content Requirements Policy](https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/9489299950487-App-Directory-App-Content-Requirements-Policy)
**Last Updated:** October 9, 2025

### Key Requirements
- **Eligibility for Discovery**: To be listed in the App Directory, your Application must:
  - Comply with Discord’s Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, Developer Terms of Service, and Developer Policy.
  - Have a **publicly available Privacy Policy and Terms of Service**.
  - Avoid age-restricted content (e.g., sexually explicit material, violence, illegal goods, gambling).
  - Ensure the Application name, description, and commands do not violate intellectual property rights.
- **Enabling Discovery**: After meeting eligibility requirements and verification, you can enable discovery in the Discord Developer Portal under the **Discovery** tab.

## 4. App Directory Inclusion Guidelines
**Source:** [App Directory Inclusion Guidelines](https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/8852009977879-App-Directory-Inclusion-Guidelines)
**Last Updated:** October 9, 2025

### Key Requirements
- **Compliance**: Applications must comply with the Developer Policy and Developer Terms of Service.
- **Content Restrictions**: Applications must not host graphic, sexual, or age-restricted content. Apps must be appropriate for audiences aged 13+.
- **Intellectual Property**: Respect intellectual property rights. Only use content you own or have a license to use.
- **Discovery Eligibility**: Applications must meet all requirements listed in the Developer Portal under **Your Application > Discovery > Discovery Status**.

---

## 5. Privacy Policy and Terms of Service Requirements
### Privacy Policy
- Must clearly state:
  - What data is collected from users (e.g., Discord IDs, server information, message content).
  - How data is used (e.g., for bot functionality, analytics, or monetization).
  - How data is stored and protected.
  - Whether data is shared with third parties (e.g., for analytics or payment processing).
  - User rights (e.g., how to request data deletion or opt out of data collection).
  - Contact information for privacy-related inquiries.

### Terms of Service
- Must include:
  - A clear description of the bot’s functionality.
  - User responsibilities (e.g., compliance with Discord’s Terms of Service).
  - Prohibited uses of the bot (e.g., spam, harassment, illegal activities).
  - Liability disclaimers (e.g., bot is provided "as is").
  - Termination clauses (e.g., Discord or the developer may terminate access for violations).
  - Contact information for support or legal inquiries.

---

## 6. Summary of Actions Required for Compliance
1. **Draft a Privacy Policy** that complies with Discord’s requirements and clearly discloses data collection, usage, storage, and user rights.
2. **Draft Terms of Service** that outline the bot’s functionality, user responsibilities, prohibited uses, and liability disclaimers.
3. **Host both documents publicly** (e.g., on a website or GitHub Pages) and link them in the Discord Developer Portal.
4. **Ensure the bot complies with all Discord policies** (e.g., no age-restricted content, no unsolicited DMs, no illegal activities).
5. **Enable discovery** in the Discord Developer Portal after verification and compliance checks.
